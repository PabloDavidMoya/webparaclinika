The method

The 6 Rs.
Five from functional medicine
and one of our own.

  1. Identify and take out the foods and factors the body doesn't tolerate well and that keep the inflammatory response going.

  2. Restore what is missing so digestion and nutrient absorption can work again.

  3. Rebuild the gut flora, where close to 70 % of the immune system lives.

  4. Support the recovery of the intestinal lining with the same nutrients the body itself uses.

  5. Put rest, stress and daily habits back in order: the autonomic nervous system governs metabolism around the clock.

  6. So the patient understands their own body and can sustain it without depending on us. Not medication: education.
